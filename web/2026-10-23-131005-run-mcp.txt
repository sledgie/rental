❯ I am getting a "server responded with a status of 500" when doing a signup    
  with my SMTP settings with google                                             

● A 500 on sign-up has two likely causes: the email can't be sent, or the
  database trigger that creates the profile fails. I can't see your Supabase
  logs from here, so I can't tell you which one it is yet.

  Find the exact error first
  Open Supabase → Logs → Auth Logs (or the Logs Explorer) and look at the failed
  /signup request. The message there will be one of these:
  - "Error sending confirmation email" (or similar): SMTP is the problem.
  - "Database error saving new user": the handle_new_user trigger from migration
    002 is failing. That's a database problem, not email.

  If it's SMTP (most common with Google)
  1. Use an App Password, not your Google password. Google blocks normal
     passwords over SMTP. Turn on 2-Step Verification, then create an App
     Password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords and paste the 16 characters
     with no spaces.
  2. Use these settings:
     - Host: smtp.gmail.com
     - Port: 465. Supabase handles 465 more reliably than 587.
     - Username: your full Gmail address.
     - Sender email: the same address, or an alias you've verified in Gmail's
       "Send mail as" settings.
  3. Workspace accounts: your Google Workspace admin must allow SMTP and app
     passwords. If they don't, Google rejects the login.
  4. Template problem: save the email template again. A stray character in the
     template can also produce a 500. To test, temporarily switch the body to
     plain text and sign up again. If that works, the template is the problem.
  5. Rate limit: Gmail caps sending at roughly 500 emails a day for personal
     accounts, and Supabase throttles auth emails separately. Repeated retries
     can trip either limit.

  To rule SMTP in or out quickly
  Turn custom SMTP off for one test. If sign-up then works, with Supabase's
  built-in sender or with "Confirm email" disabled, the problem is your SMTP
  settings and not the app.

  If the log shows the database error, paste it here. I'd check the trigger and
  the profiles / user_preferences inserts, which are in my area. Otherwise paste
  the Auth log line and I'll tell you which setting to fix.
